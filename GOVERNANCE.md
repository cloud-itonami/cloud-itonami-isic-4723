# Governance

cloud-itonami-isic-4723 follows open-source governance with transparent
decision-making documented in ADRs.

Scope: Sales-record (inventory/sale/return) logging, floor-staff
scheduling proposals, supply-order coordination, and compliance-concern
flagging (suspected age-verification failures, regulatory concerns) for
specialized tobacco retail stores (tobacconist, cigar lounge, pipe &
tobacco shop, vape specialty retailer).

Out-of-scope: Finalizing an age-verification override, directly
operating/controlling an age-verification or ID-scanning terminal, and
tobacco-retail-license suspension/revocation or other licensing-authority
enforcement (inspection clearance, excise/customs enforcement, compliance
enforcement).

See README.md for complete CRITICAL scope exclusions.
